artist profile

     Hi, I’m Lisa FLY — a Ukrainian DJ from Donbas, based in Bali for over 7 years.

I’ve been digging for new music for as long as I can remember, and I adore sharing what excites me and seeing it spark something in other people. In my sets, I prefer to follow the vibe and the groove, guided by musical curiosity rather than strict genre boundaries. My selection is wide-ranging, but house is at the heart of what I play — from deep, jazzy, disco and Italo to Detroit, acid, progressive and tech house, alongside breakbeat, electro, techno and textural leftfield electronic sounds. I’m drawn to tracks with character, story, emotion and sometimes a little bit of surprise.

    For me, DJing is about adding to the energy of a moment — amplifying a good one, shifting it when needed, and letting the music take people somewhere a little deeper. That’s why my sets are intuitive, shaped by the context, the atmosphere and the people — warm, jazzy and chill for a golden-hour sunset, playful and groovy for a daytime crowd, or more driving and trippy when the night calls for it. Whether it’s a beach club, private event, festival, bar or underground dancefloor, I want people to feel connected to the present moment, enjoy their experience, and leave feeling like the music made it even better.
